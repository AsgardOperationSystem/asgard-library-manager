Library Manager

Manage the games on your Thor from this browser. On the Thor, Settings → Hardware → Network → Library Manager shows its address.

From this website, Chrome and Edge ask once to reach devices on your network: allow it. Firefox and Safari can't, so they open the Thor's own copy.
